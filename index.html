Mushroom Shape Sitting Structure

A circular sitting structure: an RCC mushroom-shaped top slab on a central RCC column, surrounded by a brick masonry sitting wall on a PCC base. Built as a civil engineering project at Dr. C.V. Raman University, Vaishali, Bihar.

RCCBrick masonryQuality testing

Materials

  • Red clay bricks
  • OPC 53 grade cement
  • Fine sand
  • Fe-500 TMT bars, 8 mm and 12 mm
  • PCC base, mix 1:4:8

Reinforcement

  • Main bars: 12 mm
  • Stirrups and chain: 8 mm
  • Toad cap: 8 mm

Construction process

  1. Site layout and marking
  2. Excavation and sand filling
  3. PCC base
  4. Reinforcement fixing
  5. Circular wall masonry
  6. RCC column casting
  7. Mushroom top slab casting
  8. Plastering and curing

Tests carried out

  • Bricks: size, strength, water absorption
  • Cement: fineness, consistency, setting time
  • Sand: sieve analysis, silt content, bulking
  • Steel: tensile, bend and yield strength
  • Concrete: slump and cube test
